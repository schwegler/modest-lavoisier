💡 **What:** The optimization implemented is an `O(1)` index for Wiki link resolution via `this.pageNamesIndex`.

🎯 **Why:** The previous logic inside `WikiFlowApp.resolveWikiLink` used an `O(N)` loop (`this.pages.entries()`) to look for flat page matches. This resulted in significant CPU overhead during heavily repetitive calls within `getGraphNodes` and `getGraphLinks`.

📊 **Measured Improvement:** On a synthesized benchmark of 10,000 pages resolving 1,000 links, this optimization improved execution time from ~271ms down to ~1.7ms.
